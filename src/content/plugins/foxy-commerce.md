Foxy Commerce connects your Framer designs to a Foxy store without any code. Select a button or link on your canvas, open the plugin, and fill in the product details - name, price, image, and more - straight from a single panel.

Foxy itself is a headless ecommerce platform, which means it handles the cart, checkout, and payments behind the scenes while you keep full control over how your storefront actually looks. There's no template to fight against, so your site can stay exactly as you designed it.

## Key features and benefits:

- No-code setup: turn any linkable element into a "buy" button by configuring it through the plugin panel, no scripting required
- Full product control: set the name, price, SKU, and image URL for each item you sell
- Subscriptions built in: enable recurring payments with custom frequency, start dates, and end dates
- Smart cart behaviour: decide what happens before and after a product gets added to the cart
- Quantity rules: set default, minimum, and maximum quantities per product
- Coupons and discounts: apply automatic discount codes at checkout
- Shipping-ready: add weight and dimensions so shipping costs calculate correctly
- 100+ payment gateways: including Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, Klarna, and many more

Because Foxy is headless, none of the checkout logic gets in the way of your design. You build the look in Framer, and Foxy quietly takes care of carts, taxes, shipping, and secure payments in the background.

This makes Foxy Commerce a solid pick for designers who want a genuinely custom online store - one that doesn't feel like it was bolted on from a generic ecommerce template - without needing to write a single line of code to wire it up.

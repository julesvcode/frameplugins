Mapframe turns your Framer CMS into an interactive map. Drop in the component, point it at a collection, and every item becomes a marker with its own popup – no map libraries and no code required.

## Key features and benefits:

- CMS or manual points: connect a Framer CMS collection, or type locations straight into the property panel if you don't need a CMS at all.
- Custom markers and popups: design markers and popups as Framer components so they match your brand instead of looking like a generic map embed.
- Clustering and hover tooltips: keep busy maps readable, with clusters and hover states that stay tidy as collections grow.
- 17 map themes: pick from light, dark, and everything in between, then style the rest of the map from the property panel.
- Globe, 3D buildings, and camera animations: switch between globe and flat projections, add 3D buildings, and fly between locations.
- Arcs, routes, and GeoJSON overlays: draw connections, multi-stop routes, and area overlays without leaving Framer.
- One-time payment: pay once and use it on your site, with no per-view fees or ongoing subscription.
